Privacy Policy

Barnaby's Tavern: Idle Heroes (package com.tavernidle.game)
Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Barnaby's Tavern: Idle Heroes ("the game", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer. If you have any questions, contact us at dinc.yusuf.business@gmail.com.

1. Information the game stores on your device

Your game progress (heroes, items, waves, settings) is saved on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive this data.

2. Google Play Games Services

The game can use Google Play Games Services to sign you in automatically and to back up your progress with Saved Games. When you use these features, Google processes your Play Games profile (such as your gamer name and avatar) and stores your saved game in your Google account. We only use this to restore your progress on your devices. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in to Play Games.

3. In-app purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, they are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details. We receive only the purchase information needed to deliver your items, such as an order ID and the product bought.

4. Advertising

If the game shows ads, they are provided by a Google Play certified ad network (for example Google AdMob). The ad network may collect device information such as your advertising ID, IP address and approximate location to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Where required by law, the game will ask for your consent before ads are personalized. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Crash and diagnostics data

Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash reports and performance statistics through the Play Console. These do not identify you personally.

6. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described above (Google Play, Google Play Games Services and, if ads are shown, the ad network) so they can provide their services.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local progress is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Saved Games stored with Google Play Games can be deleted from your Google account in the Google Play Games app (Settings > Delete Play Games account and data) or by contacting us. To request deletion of any other data, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its processing. Contact us at the email above to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
