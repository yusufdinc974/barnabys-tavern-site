Delete your data

Barnaby's Tavern: Idle Heroes (developer: Sicakahve)

Game progress on your device

Your progress is stored only on your phone. To delete it, uninstall the game or go to Settings > Apps > Barnaby's Tavern > Storage > Clear data.

Cloud saves (Google Play Games)

If you signed in with Google Play Games, your saved game is stored in your Google account. To delete it:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app.
  2. Go to Settings (or your profile) > Delete Play Games account and data.
  3. Choose to delete the data for Barnaby's Tavern: Idle Heroes (or all games).

Request deletion by email

You can also email dinc.yusuf.business@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete any data related to you that we can access and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted and kept

Deletion removes your game progress and cloud save. We do not keep a copy. Purchase records kept by Google Play for legal and tax reasons are handled by Google and are not deleted by us.

See also our Privacy Policy.
